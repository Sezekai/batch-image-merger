Batch Image Merger
==================

Unzip this into  E:\Pictures\Batch Image Merger  so that you get

    E:\Pictures\Batch Image Merger\App\Batch Image Merger.exe

and start it from there.

Where things live
    App\        the program. Replaced by each update; nothing else is touched.
    Cache\      thumbnails, cutouts and previews (made beside App the first time; safe to clear).
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Batch Image Merger
                your sets, settings, presets, look, themes, fonts and logs.

To update: close the app, then replace the App folder with the one from the new zip.
Settings → Storage shows every place with its size, and can open, move or clear them.

Press K in the app for the keyboard shortcuts.
